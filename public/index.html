Questions

Before you ask…

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You fill in a form, see a preview, and we handle the rest. If you pick done-for-you, you don't even do that.

What does the setup fee cover?

It covers registering your .org domain for the first year, connecting it to your site, and getting everything live. The monthly fee starts after that.

What exactly do I get for ₦10,000 a month?

Your live website, hosting, updates, support, and the WhatsApp ordering flow. You can change your prices and services whenever you like. Domain renewal after year one is billed separately at cost.

How fast will my site be live?

The mockup is instant. With Launch, your site can be live the same day. Done-for-you takes about 7 days.

I already have a domain — can I use it?

Yes. Tell us your domain and we'll point it at your site. Let us know before paying the setup fee so we can adjust it.

Will it work on phones?

Yes — it's built mobile-first, because that's where your customers are.
